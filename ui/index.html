VideoFitAI

Is this video right for you?

Paste a YouTube link and its transcript. VideoFit scores how well the video fits your learning goal, and lets you ask it questions with clickable timestamps.

Topic checklist (optional)

One topic per line. Leave empty to let the model build a checklist from your goal. Writing your own makes repeated scores comparable.

Audience comments (optional)

One comment per line. They are summarized separately and never change the score.

Get the transcript from YouTube

  1. Open the video on a computer.
  2. Under the video, click …more in the description, then Show transcript.
  3. In the transcript panel, open the ⋮ menu and make sure Toggle timestamps is on.
  4. Select all the transcript lines, copy them, and paste them here.

Timestamps are what make the video links work. Without them you can still chat, but answers cannot link to a time.

Good to know

  • The score is a heuristic, not a verdict.
  • "Not detected" means not found in your pasted text.
  • Your transcript and comments are sent to the Groq API to produce results. Nothing else leaves your computer.
